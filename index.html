Our Philosophy

Founded in 2019 by certified fitness professionals, our mission is to make evidence-based fitness coaching accessible to everyone, regardless of experience level or budget. Our team holds certifications from NASM, ACE, NSCA, and other recognized organizations.

We reject the "quick fix" mentality that dominates the fitness industry. Instead, we focus on building sustainable habits, proper movement patterns, and a healthy relationship with exercise and nutrition. Our approach is grounded in exercise science and sports medicine research.

With 500+ members trusting our programs, we are committed to individual attention and measurable results. Our trainers undergo continuing education annually and stay current with the latest research in exercise science and nutrition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our services, policies, and processes.

Do I need gym equipment?

No. We offer programs for home workouts with minimal or no equipment, as well as full gym programs. During onboarding, we ask about your equipment access to design an appropriate program.

Can I do this program if I have a medical condition?

We recommend getting medical clearance from your physician before starting any exercise program. Our trainers can work within guidelines provided by your healthcare provider, but we do not provide medical advice or replace physical therapy.

Will I lose weight on this program?

We cannot guarantee specific weight loss results, as outcomes depend on many factors including nutrition, consistency, sleep, stress, and individual physiology. We focus on building sustainable habits, improving fitness markers, and overall well-being.

How often should I work out?

We typically recommend 3-5 sessions per week depending on your goals and fitness level, but programs are customized. Consistency matters more than frequency, and we design programs that fit realistically into your life.

Are your trainers certified?

Yes, all trainers hold nationally recognized certifications (NASM, ACE, NSCA, or equivalent) and maintain current CPR/AED certification. Many hold additional specializations in areas like corrective exercise, nutrition, and sports performance.
